This is the similarity rate. It measures how alike the two recipes are overall, from 0 to 100%, weighing each setting by how much it differs and how much that setting matters to the look. For example, a change to the film simulation counts for far more than a tweak to noise reduction.

Identity is the closeness of the core look (film simulation, white balance, tone and colour); it sets the ceiling. Extras is the closeness of the secondary settings (dynamic range, grain, sharpness, noise), which can only nudge the score within that ceiling. Full method in ADR 019.
